Privacy Policy for Lead Saver - Save Leads to CSV

Effective Date: November 24, 2026

Lead Saver is a Chrome extension that lets you bookmark webpages as leads with personal notes and export your list to a local CSV file.

Data Collection and Storage

Lead Saver does not collect, transmit, sell, or share any personal data or browsing history with the developer or any third party. All saved lead names, URLs, notes, and timestamps are stored exclusively on your own device using Chrome's local storage API (chrome.storage.local).

Permissions Used

activeTab: Accessed only when you click the extension icon to prefill the current tab's title and URL.
storage: Used strictly to persist your saved leads locally between browser sessions until you export or delete them.

Support & Questions

For questions or feedback, please open an issue on the GitHub repository.
